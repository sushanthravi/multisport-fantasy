# Multisport Fantasy

Fantasy sports are usually played in separate apps and leagues. If your friends follow more than one sport, there is no single place to compete together across them.

**Multisport Fantasy is a fantasy league that brings different sports into one game.** Friends draft a team of athletes, earn points from real performances, and compete on one leaderboard. The goal is to make following several sports more fun and give every sport a fair impact on the result.

We are starting with Premier League football, NFL, and Formula 1. More sports can be added as the game grows.

The current version lets you explore athletes and try the draft. We are working toward a shared online league where friends can draft from their own devices and follow scores as games and races happen.
